TorqueWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Summary

  • TorqueWise collects no personal information. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics, and it does not track you.
  • The joints you set up and save stay on your device. TorqueWise never uploads them, and we do not receive them.
  • The app makes no internet connections of its own. Buying and restoring TorqueWise Pro go through Apple.
  • Apple handles the payment for TorqueWise Pro. We never see your name, email address, or payment details from a purchase.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and what you send.

This policy explains what information the TorqueWise app for iPhone and iPad (“TorqueWise” or “the app”) and this website, torquewise.journeyon.co, handle, and what choices you have. We are responsible for the information described here; in European and UK law we are the “controller”. Questions: support@journeyon.co.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Your inputs and saved joints. TorqueWise remembers the joint you set up last and the joints you save with Save joint: the name you give a joint, its bolt and joint settings, its torque, and the date you saved it. They are stored in the app’s storage on your device, with a copy in the app’s preferences so that they are kept if iOS clears the app’s web storage. We do not receive them.
  • Calculations. Every calculation runs on your device. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Copy callout. Copy callout puts the callout text on your device’s clipboard. It goes only where you paste it.
  • TorqueWise Pro. The app learns from iOS whether your Apple Account has bought TorqueWise Pro (section 2). We do not receive this.
  • Backups. The app’s saved joints are included in iCloud and computer backups of your iPhone or iPad, under Apple’s or your computer’s terms. Deleting the app deletes them from the device.
  • No other access. TorqueWise does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts, your photos, or Apple Health, and it asks for no permissions. The app gives a light vibration when you pick an option; this uses no information.

We do not send your inputs or saved joints to any other company.

2. TorqueWise Pro and Apple

TorqueWise Pro is bought in the app through Apple. Apple processes the payment and manages your Apple Account under Apple’s Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Apple does not give us your name, email address, or payment details, and the app sends nothing about the purchase to us. App Store Connect gives us totals, such as the number of purchases, that do not identify you.

3. This website

  • Hosting. The pages at torquewise.journeyon.co are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When your browser asks for a page or file, GitHub receives the technical details that come with any internet request, such as your IP address and browser type, and may log the IP address for security.
  • No cookies or analytics. The pages use no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party scripts, fonts, or other resources. Everything they load comes from this website.

4. Support emails

When you email us, we receive your email address, any name your email includes, and what you send. We use it only to answer you. We keep support emails for 24 months after the last message, then delete them. Our email is hosted by Google (Google Workspace).

5. Information Apple may give us

If you allow it in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > Share With App Developers), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics for the app. App Store Connect also gives us totals such as downloads. Apple provides these under its own privacy policy, and they do not identify you to us.

6. Links to other websites

Sources and method in TorqueWise lists the standards and references behind the results, with links to their publishers’ websites, and the app links to this website’s Support, Privacy Policy, and Terms pages. A link opens in your browser, and the privacy policy of the website it opens applies there. The footer of this website links to our Instagram page, where Instagram’s privacy policy applies.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law defines these terms.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising identifiers, work with data brokers, or track you as Apple defines tracking.
  • We do not use analytics tools that record how you use TorqueWise.
  • Because we do not track you, TorqueWise has no setting for “Do Not Track” signals.

8. Legal bases (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

  • Support emails: to answer your request.
  • The host’s logs (section 3): our legitimate interest in keeping this website secure and available.
  • Your inputs and saved joints: TorqueWise processes them only on your device, for you, and we do not receive them.
  • TorqueWise Pro: we receive no personal information from a purchase; Apple processes it under its own privacy policy.

9. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it or delete the app. Copies in device backups stay until those backups are deleted.
  • Support emails: as in section 4.
  • The host’s logs: under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Apple’s records: under Apple’s privacy policy.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a saved joint. Open Saved, tap Delete next to the joint, then tap Confirm.
  • Delete everything. Delete the app.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. The only personal information we hold is in support emails. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority. Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws have similar rights, and we will not treat you differently for using them. To use a right, email support@journeyon.co. We answer within the time the law sets, and we will not ask for more information than we need.

11. Children

TorqueWise is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. TorqueWise sends no information from the device to us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Information on your device is protected by iOS. This website is served over encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is completely secure.

13. International users

We are based in the United States. If you email us from another country, your email is transferred to the United States. Transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are made under standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for a copy at support@journeyon.co.

14. Changes to this policy

We post changes on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If a change affects what TorqueWise collects, we say so in the app’s update notes and on this website before it takes effect, and we update the App Store privacy information.
