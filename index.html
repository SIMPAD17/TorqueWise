TorqueWise

Bolt torque and preload calculator.

Pick the bolt, the joint and how you tighten it. TorqueWise gives the tightening torque, the preload it gives and its expected range, the checks that go with it, and the tightening sequence for the bolt pattern. A 3D view shows the bolt in a cut-away of the joint.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later. In English.

What it covers

  • Bolts. Metric M3–M36 and inch #4 to 1-1/2 in, coarse and fine threads. Hex, heavy hex, socket head, low head, flange, button, countersunk and pan head screws, carriage bolts and studs.
  • Grades and finishes. Property classes 4.6 to 12.9, A2-70, A4-70 and A4-80; SAE J429 Grades 1, 2, 5 and 8, ASTM F3125 A325 and A490, A574, A193 B7 and F593. Zinc, yellow zinc, plain, black oxide, zinc flake, hot-dip galvanized, PTFE and stainless; as received, oiled, with MoS₂ paste or anti-seize, or a friction value you measured.
  • Joints. Through bolt and nut, or tapped hole, in steel, stainless steel, cast iron, aluminum, magnesium, titanium or a material you describe, with washers and lock nuts.
  • Preload targets. A share of the proof load, the VDI 2230 limit, gasket flanges (ASME PCC-1) and structural bolting (RCSC Specification and EN 1090-2).
  • Checks. Bearing pressure under the head, thread engagement, bolt length, corrosion between metals and lock nuts, each with its reference.
  • Sequence. The tightening passes, lettered A to D, and the bolt order for patterns of up to 24 bolts.
  • Units. N·m and kN, or ft·lb, in·lb and lbf.

The method

The torque and preload follow VDI 2230 Part 1 (2015), with the dimensions and strengths of the product and material standards. Sources and method in the app lists every source, and runs 27 checks against published values each time it opens: VDI 2230 Table A1, Fastenal clamp-load tables, RCSC Table 5.2, EN 1090-2 preload values, ISO 2320 and others.

The results are estimates from published methods. Where the manufacturer specifies a torque or a tightening procedure, that comes first, and joints whose failure could cause harm need a qualified engineer (Terms of Use, section 4).

Free and TorqueWise Pro

TorqueWise is free to download. Free: every bolt and joint input, the set torque, the preload with its expected range and gauge, the Standard preload target, the 3D view and the section drawing, every note of the checks, and the standards and sources.

TorqueWise Pro is a one-time purchase in the app. It adds the VDI 2230 limit, gasket flange and structural targets, the check tiles, where the torque goes, the tightening sequence, the drawing callout, the full calculation, and saved joints.

Privacy

TorqueWise collects no personal information. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it works without an internet connection. The Privacy Policy has the details.
