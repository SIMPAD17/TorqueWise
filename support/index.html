What is TorqueWise?

A bolt torque and preload calculator for iPhone and iPad. Pick the bolt (size, head, grade, finish, and thread condition), the joint (through bolt or tapped hole, clamped material, grip, washer, and nut), and the preload target and tool. TorqueWise gives the tightening torque, the preload and its expected range, checks of bearing pressure, thread engagement, bolt length, corrosion, and lock nuts, and the tightening sequence for the bolt pattern. A 3D view shows the bolt in a cut-away of the joint. It is free to download, and TorqueWise Pro is a one-time purchase in the app.

What is free, and what does TorqueWise Pro add?

Free: every bolt and joint input, the set torque, the preload with its expected range and gauge, the Standard preload target, the 3D view and the section drawing, every note of the checks, and the standards used and Sources and method. TorqueWise Pro adds the VDI 2230 limit, Gasket flange, and Structural preload targets, the check tiles, where the torque goes, the tightening sequence, the drawing callout and Copy callout, Show the calculation, and saved joints.

How much does TorqueWise Pro cost?

In the United States, US$9.99, once. There is no subscription. Prices differ in other countries; the app shows the price for your country before you confirm.

How do I turn on TorqueWise Pro on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, tap TorqueWise Pro at the bottom of the app’s page, and tap Restore purchase. Do the same after you install the app again. Family Sharing is not available for TorqueWise Pro.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds. A refund turns TorqueWise Pro off in the app.

Which standards does TorqueWise follow?

The torque and preload follow VDI 2230 Part 1 (2015). The grades’ strengths come from their material standards, such as ISO 898-1 and SAE J429, and the dimensions of bolts, nuts, washers, and holes from their product standards. The structural targets follow the RCSC Specification and EN 1090-2, and the gasket flange target and the cross-pattern tightening sequence follow ASME PCC-1. Sources and method in TorqueWise lists every source and how each one is used.

How accurate are the results?

The calculation is checked against published values: VDI 2230 Table A1, Fastenal clamp-load tables, RCSC Table 5.2, EN 1090-2 preload values, and ISO 2320. Sources and method runs these 27 checks each time you open it and shows each result. The largest uncertainty in a real joint is friction: the same torque gives a different preload when the friction changes, and friction varies with the finish, the lubricant and how it is applied, the batch, and the tool. TorqueWise shows the expected preload range for the tool you choose. Where preload matters, measure the friction of your parts (ISO 16047) and enter it, or measure the preload directly. The Terms of Use list what the calculation leaves out.

Why does the torque differ from a torque chart?

A chart is worked out for one friction value or nut factor, one preload target, and one kind of head and washer. TorqueWise works the torque out from the finish, the thread condition, the head, the washer, and the preload target you choose. To compare with a chart, set the same thread condition and preload target as the chart, for example Standard at 75 % of proof load.

The manufacturer gives a different torque. Which one do I use?

The manufacturer’s. Where the maker of the equipment, vehicle, or fastener specifies a torque, a tightening procedure, or that a bolt must be replaced, follow their specification. For joints whose failure could hurt someone, the joint and its tightening are a qualified engineer’s decision, under the code or standard that applies (Terms of Use, section 4).

Which units does TorqueWise use?

Metric bolts give the torque in N·m and the preload in kN. Inch bolts give the torque in ft·lb, or in in·lb for small torques, and the preload in lbf. Under the main torque, TorqueWise also shows it in the other system’s unit.

Where are my saved joints stored?

On your device only. They are stored in the app and included in iCloud and computer backups of your device. TorqueWise has no account and does not sync saved joints between devices.

How do I delete my saved joints?

Open Saved, tap Delete next to a joint, then tap Confirm. To delete everything, delete the app.

Does TorqueWise work offline?

Yes. The calculator works without a connection. Buying and restoring TorqueWise Pro need one.

What does TorqueWise do with my information?

Nothing you enter leaves your device. TorqueWise collects no personal information and has no account, no ads, and no analytics, and Apple handles the purchase of TorqueWise Pro. The Privacy Policy has the details.
